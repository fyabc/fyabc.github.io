Yang Fan

Algorithm Expert at Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group.

Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China

I am an Algorithm Expert at Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group, working on large language models as a member of the Qwen team. My work covers LLM training, fine-tuning, and data engineering, contributing to the Qwen series including Qwen, Qwen2, Qwen3, Qwen2-VL, Qwen2.5-Omni, and Qwen2.5-Coder.

Before joining Alibaba, I was a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia (2016–2022), mentored by Dr. Tao Qin. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2022.

My research interests span large language models, learning to teach (meta-learning frameworks that guide model training with a teacher model), neural architecture search, and neural machine translation. Our team won championship in 8 translation tasks at WMT19. See the publications page for the full list.

In my spare time I build hobby projects: Dreamulator, a science-grounded fantasy world simulator, and Oort, a toy programming language for exploring compiler design.
